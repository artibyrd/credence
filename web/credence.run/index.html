The Automated Truth & Deception Engine for AI Agents and the Web

Credence audits web pages, news articles, and research for clickbait, logical fallacies, journalistic ethics violations, and manipulative dark patterns — producing cryptographically signed, zero-hallucination truth attestations that AI agents and humans can verify in real time.

bash — quickstart
curl -fsSL https://credence.run/install.sh | bash
credence audit https://example.com/breaking-news
credence serve --mcp # Connect live to Claude, Cursor & Antigravity

How Credence Works

1. 4-Specialist Multi-Agent Audit

Automated specialist agents capture rendered DOM trees, cleaned prose, and full-page screenshots to audit content against formal, namespaced taxonomies (SPJ Journalistic Ethics, IEP Logical Fallacies, and Deceptive UI Patterns).

2. Zero-Hallucination Grounded Citations

Every identified violation strictly requires an exact verbatim character-matched quote from the source DOM. Fabricated or hallucinated citations instantly slash the evaluator's reputation score by 50%.

3. Native FastMCP 2.0 for AI Agents

Give Claude, Cursor, and autonomous agent swarms the real-time ability to evaluate URLs, inspect citations, and query consensus trust scores over standard I/O and Server-Sent Events (SSE) before citing sources.

4. Decentralized Cryptographic Mesh

Audits are signed with Ed25519 keys into deterministic RFC 8785 envelopes and gossiped across a 13-node Watts-Strogatz small-world lattice with Byzantine cartel resistance (N ≥ 3f + 1).

5. The Galileo Rule (Anti-Censorship)

Absence of evidence is not evidence of absence. Grounded domain authorities submitting 100% verifiable citations cannot be censored or out-voted by dishonest consensus swarms.

6. Work-Sharing & Zero-Token Adoptions

BitTorrent-inspired syndicated feed distribution: when one node audits a breaking article, all mesh peers verify and adopt the signed attestation at $0.00 compute cost.
