Architecture

How Credence Works

From raw webpage capture to cryptographically signed truth attestations in 4 clear steps.

1

Capture & Verbatim Grounding

Headless Chromium captures rendered DOM, cleaned prose, and screenshots. Every finding strictly requires an exact verbatim character match from the source text ($G = 1.0$) — eliminating AI hallucinations entirely.

2

Multi-Taxonomy Specialist Audit

Specialist agents evaluate content against pinned taxonomy catalogs: SPJ Journalistic Ethics (unnamed sources, unverified allegations), Logical Fallacies (Ad Hominem, Straw Man), and Deceptive UI Patterns.

3

Ed25519 Cryptographic Envelope

The node canonicalizes the audit into RFC 8785 JSON, signs it with its Ed25519 private key, and gossips the attestation across the P2P mesh network for instant zero-token adoption ($0.00 compute cost) by peer nodes.

4

The Galileo Rule & Swarm Defense

Absence of evidence is not evidence of absence. Verified domain authorities submitting 100% grounded citations cannot be silenced by dishonest swarms, and rogue nodes are autonomously slashed.
