IEP-1.1 Sev 3 Ad Hominem (Abusive / Circumstantial)

Attacking an opponent's character, background, appearance, or motives instead of engaging with their substantive argument.
IEP-1.2 Sev 3 Tu Quoque / Whataboutism
🔬 Find Real Examples ↗
Deflecting criticism by accusing the accuser of hypocrisy or raising unrelated counter-allegations.
IEP-1.3 Sev 4 Poisoning the Well
🔬 Find Real Examples ↗
Preemptively presenting adverse information about an opponent to discredit anything they might say beforehand.
IEP-1.4 Sev 2 Genetic Fallacy
🔬 Find Real Examples ↗
Judging the validity of an idea purely on the basis of its origin or source history rather than its merits.
IEP-2.1 Sev 3 Begging the Question (Petitio Principii)
🔬 Find Real Examples ↗
An argument whose premises assume the truth of the conclusion they are supposed to prove.
IEP-2.2 Sev 3 False Dilemma / Forced Dichotomy
🔬 Find Real Examples ↗
Presenting complex, nuanced situations as an either/or choice while ignoring viable middle grounds.
IEP-2.3 Sev 3 Loaded Question
🔬 Find Real Examples ↗
Asking a question containing an unproven, incriminating presumption that cannot be answered simply without admitting guilt.
IEP-2.4 Sev 4 Cherry-Picking (Texas Sharpshooter)
🔬 Find Real Examples ↗
Highlighting only confirming evidence or favorable outliers while ignoring vast contradictory data.
IEP-3.1 Sev 3 Post Hoc Ergo Propter Hoc
🔬 Find Real Examples ↗
Assuming that because Event B occurred after Event A, Event A must have caused Event B.
IEP-3.2 Sev 3 Correlation as Causation
🔬 Find Real Examples ↗
Treating statistical correlation between two variables as definitive proof that one causes the other.
IEP-3.3 Sev 2 Hasty Generalization
🔬 Find Real Examples ↗
Drawing a broad conclusion from a sample that is too small or unrepresentative.
IEP-3.4 Sev 3 Slippery Slope
🔬 Find Real Examples ↗
Claiming that a minor initial step will inevitably trigger a disastrous chain reaction without proving each link.
IEP-4.1 Sev 4 Appeal to Fear (In Terrorem)
🔬 Find Real Examples ↗

IEP-4.2 Sev 3 Appeal to False Authority
🔬 Find Real Examples ↗

IEP-5.1 Sev 4 Straw Man Argument
🔬 Find Real Examples ↗
